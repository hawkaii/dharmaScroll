## Findings

- Everything matched except Janmashtami 2027 for New Delhi. We give 24 Aug (Ashtami covers midnight of 24-25 Aug); the pages we could read give 25 Aug, which fits the Vaishnava or Rohini rule. The nishita rule for Smarta/Vaishnava Janmashtami is not implemented, so Janmashtami is shipped flagged unverified (`UNVERIFIED_FESTIVALS` in generate.py): the app neither shows nor schedules it.
- Guru Purnima first failed for Fairfield (we said 17 Jul, Drik says 18 Jul). Fixed by using the tithi at sunrise; rerun matches in Delhi and Fairfield.
- Purnima vrat first failed for Pausha 2027; fixed by using the tithi at moonrise instead of sunset.
- Tithi boundaries agree with Drik to within 1-2 minutes in every row, so remaining risk is the day-choosing rule, not the astronomy. Parana start differs by up to 1 minute (sunrise rounding).
- Not audited: Pradosh, Sankashti, Amavasya (date), other festivals, and every city other than Delhi, Edison, New York and Fairfield. Those are produced by the same rules.
- Ekadashi that lies wholly between two sunrises (Delhi, 20 Nov 2026) follows the Drik date: Smarta on the day it prevails, Vaishnava on the next day.
