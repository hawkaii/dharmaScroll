### Full description (max 4000)

```
dharmaScroll is a quiet daily habit built around the Bhagavad Gita.

Each day brings five verses. You hear them, you chant with them, you say a few words of your own. Then the app tells you that you may rest. It does not ask for more, and it does not try to keep you scrolling.

The daily ritual

Hear. Take each verse slowly in Sanskrit, with its transliteration and a plain meaning. Verses are always attributed to the one speaking, for example Krishna speaking to Arjuna.

Walk. Hold the button and chant along with the verse.

Speak. Say a few words about your day in your own voice. The app plays it back to you. Your recording stays on your phone.

Keep. Save any verse you want to return to.

Gita Yatra

A path through all 18 chapters of the Gita. The app remembers where you stopped and brings you to the next verse.

Streaks and points that stay kind

Your lamp is lit on each day you take part. Moksh Points only ever go up. If you miss a day, nothing is taken away from you.

Made to be calm

A dark theme and a light theme. Large, readable Sanskrit and a clear Hindi and English interface. Gentle sounds you can switch off.

Private by design

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your progress, your kept verses and your recordings stay on your phone and are never sent to us. The microphone is used only when you choose to speak or chant. You can delete everything by clearing the app's storage.

A note on respect

dharmaScroll is a reading and practice companion. It is not a guru, it is not a substitute for a teacher, and nobody in it speaks as Krishna or as God. The verses speak for themselves.

dharmaScroll is free to use. If paid features are ever added, scripture will stay free, prices will be shown with taxes included, and cancelling will be simple and inside the app.
```
