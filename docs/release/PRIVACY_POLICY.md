# Privacy Policy: dharmaScroll

Last updated: 12 November 2026

dharmaScroll is a daily Bhagavad Gita app for Android. This policy says what the app does with your information. The short version: your information stays on your phone.

## What the app does not do

- It does not ask you to make an account or sign in.
- It does not send any of your information to us or to anyone else.
- It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
- It does not sell or share data.
- It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or files.

## What stays on your phone

| What | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Your daily progress: days you took part, your streak, points, the verses you chose to keep | To show your progress and bring you back to your place | App storage on your phone |
| Your settings: language, light or dark theme, sound on or off, reminder time | To remember your choices | App storage on your phone |
| A short voice recording, if you choose to speak | To play it back to you during the daily ritual | A temporary file in the app's private cache on your phone |

## Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission only when you choose to speak or chant. The recording is made and played on your phone. It is not uploaded and it is not sent to any speech service. You can refuse the permission, or turn it off in Android settings at any time. The rest of the app works without it.

## Backups

The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so your progress and recordings are not copied to your Google account by the app.

## Children

dharmaScroll is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, Apps, dharmaScroll, Storage, and choose Clear storage. Everything the app holds is removed. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

## Changes

If a future version sends any information off your phone, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask you first.
