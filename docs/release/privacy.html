Privacy Policy: dharmaScroll

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (version 0.2.0)

dharmaScroll is a daily Bhagavad Gita app for Android. This policy says what the app does with your information. The short version: your information stays on your phone. The release app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to make an account or sign in.
  • It does not send any of your information to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or share data.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • It does not make calls or send messages by itself.

What you say or type in Mann Darpan and Ekant is matched to a verse on the phone. It is not saved unless you choose to keep it.

Microphone and speech

The app asks for microphone permission only when you choose to speak or chant. You can refuse it, or turn it off in Android settings at any time. The rest of the app works without it, and you can always type instead.

To turn speech into words, the app uses your phone's own speech service (on most phones, Google's). The app does not send audio anywhere itself. In Ekant, the app asks that service to stay on the phone, and Ekant does not listen if the offline language pack is missing. In Mann Darpan, your phone's speech service may process the audio under its own provider's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on a ritual or the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the phone; no push service is used. The app also asks Android to restore them after the phone restarts.

Calls and messages

If something you say sounds like a crisis, the app shows helpline numbers. Tapping one opens your phone's dialler or messages app with the number filled in. You decide whether to call or send. The app has no permission to call or text on its own.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a verse card, the app makes an image on the phone and opens Android's share sheet. It goes only where you send it.

Backups

The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so your progress, notes and recordings are not copied to your Google account by the app.

Children

dharmaScroll is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, Apps, dharmaScroll, Storage, and choose Clear storage. Everything the app holds is removed. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

Changes

If a future version sends any information off your phone, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask you first.
