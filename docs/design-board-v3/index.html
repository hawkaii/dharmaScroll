01 · Onboarding · 3 screens, one choiceArrive

No questionnaire. Screen 1 sets the mood, 2 shows the ritual in one line, 3 asks the single thing we need: the hour. Mic permission comes later, at the first Mann Darpan.

02 · TodayOne button

Warm dark by default, morning-puja light in the early hours (Today only). The ritual rail replaces the three dots from v2 and uses the crafted icons.

03 · Shloka scrollFive verses, then rest

Vertical snap, no icon rail, gestures only. Verse in warm gold on plum; keep is a rose lotus; listening is a peacock-and-rose wash from below.

04 · Scripture interactions · instead of a quizThree ways to walk a verse

No multiple choice, no right-or-wrong chips. Each Walk step is one of these. Default: Chant along. It is embodied, takes 40 seconds, and cannot be "failed".

Why I picked Chant along

It is what a person does with a shloka. It has no score, so nobody is judged. It builds the one skill the app promises: knowing the verse by heart and ear. II returns inside Mann Darpan replies. III becomes the Checkpoint at the end of a chapter.
