## What we take
1. Scroll the Bible proves the format. Its weakness: a social rail, ads, "Bible-as-content" feel.
2. Duolingo: mechanics, motion and mascot-state change (our "deity" is a diya/lotus/Om, not a cartoon).
3. Finch: guilt-free return. Calm: audio + silence. Opal: sparse dark badges.
4. Remove from all of them: leaderboards, comments, tabs, store, endless feeds.
