BEGIN TODAY'S RITUAL · Day 12

Inter (sans) · buttons, labels only · 10-15 px, wide tracking

Rules: no red anywhere. No card borders except selected state. Gold means "light". Teal means "your voice or the chant". Corners 18 / 28 / 42. Motion: spring cubic-bezier(.22,1.2,.36,1), 90 ms press, 380 ms snap, 3-5 s breathing.

01 · Onboarding · 3 screensArrive

No sign-up wall, no quiz, no permission storm. Three screens, three taps. Mic permission is asked later, at the first Mann Darpan, with a reason.

02 · Home · today's ritualOne button

Hear, Walk, Speak. Three quiet marks, one gold button. No feed preview, no banners, no store. Sangha is one line: a count of lamps lit today.

03 · Shloka Scroll · the TikTok replacementFive verses, then rest

One verse per screen. Vertical snap. No icon rail. Gestures do everything: hold to listen, double-tap to keep, swipe up for the next. After the fifth: "Enough for today."

04 · Mann Darpan · mirror of the mindSpeak, and a verse answers

One question, one mic, one verse back. No chat. No advice paragraph. The reply is always a real shloka and Krishna's words to Arjuna, with one small, safe thing to do.

05 · Lesson · CheckpointTwo minutes, never a quiz show

Each Yatra step is 3 cards: read, understand, recall. A Checkpoint closes each chapter. Max 3 answer choices. Wrong answers get a gentle "Look again", never a buzzer.

06 · Reward · StreakThe lamp is lit

The one big celebration of the day, shown once, after Speak. Points count up, the week's lamps light one by one. Milestones (7, 30, 100, 365) add one conch note. No confetti.

07 · Gita YatraThe path

18 chapters, one path, a node per step. You do not choose. The next node glows; the rest wait. A diamond marks each chapter Checkpoint.

08 · YouFive settings, no more

Reminder time, Language, Sound and haptics, Appearance, Privacy and data. Everything else is decided for you.

Removed from settings, on purpose

Font size · theme colours · voice choice · chant speed · verses per day · streak goal · notification categories · leaderboard opt-in · translation source.

Font size follows the system. Chant is one voice, one speed. Five verses a day. The system picks the translation.

09 · Live micro-interactionsFeel the board

Hover or press where noted. Everything is CSS; Flutter will use implicit/explicit animations with the same timings.

10 · Sound & haptic mapEvery touch answers

Sounds are soft, Indian-instrument, short (under 1.2 s), and mixed quiet (-18 LUFS bed, -12 for cues). One toggle turns sound and haptics off together. Haptics use the platform's standard patterns (selection, light impact, success), so no custom motors.
