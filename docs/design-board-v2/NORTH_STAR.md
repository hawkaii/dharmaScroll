# dharmaScroll: North Star

**One line:** a quiet, premium place that replaces your evening scroll with five verses of the Gita, and ends.

## Who it is for
A young Indian adult (18-35) who knows the Gita is "good for me", scrolls Reels for an hour, feels bad, and has never finished a chapter. Reads Hindi, English or Hinglish. Wants calm, not a lecture, and not a candy game.

## The one daily ritual (3 steps, about 6 minutes)
1. **Hear.** Five verses, one per screen. Listen, keep what lands. The feed ends.
2. **Walk.** One step on the Yatra path (a 2-minute lesson). Chapter end = a Checkpoint.
3. **Speak.** 30 seconds to the mic: "How is your mind?" A verse answers. You seal one Sankalp (a one-line vow). The diya is lit.

Home shows one button: **Begin today's ritual**. Nothing else competes.

## Five principles
1. **One thing at a time.** One verse, one button, one decision per screen.
2. **It ends.** Enough is a feature. No infinite scroll.
3. **Light, not fire.** Reward is warmth and light (diya, gold), never alarms, never red.
4. **The verse speaks.** Copy talks to you in second person, in Krishna's words from the Gita.
5. **Smooth is the product.** 60 fps, every touch answered by motion, sound and haptic in the same 100 ms.

## What we deliberately do NOT offer
- Infinite scroll, autoplay-next, "for you" ranking.
- Like / comment / share rail on verses. (Share exists only as one card from the Keep screen.)
- Leaderboards, leagues, friend ranking, public comments.
- Choosing fonts, font sizes, themes, backgrounds, voices, chanters, speeds. (We pick one.)
- Choosing how many verses per day (it is 5) or which chapter next (the path decides).
- Multiple streak types, streak freezes you must buy, hearts/energy, gems store.
- Points deductions. Points only go up.
- Push at night, guilt pushes, "you lost your streak" screens.
- A chat with "Krishna". Mann Darpan returns a verse, never free-form advice.
- Tabs with more than 3 items (Today, Yatra, You).

## "The verse speaks" voice guide
Rule: **the verse speaks, the app never claims to be God.** Always attribute: "Krishna says to Arjuna (2.47)". Second person. Warm, short, never a command about the real world, never a threat, no medical or life-decision advice.

10 example lines:
1. "Arjuna, you have a right to your work, never to its fruit. (2.47) Begin."
2. "Krishna told Arjuna: lift yourself by yourself. You are your own friend. (6.5)"
3. "You are tired. So was he. Hear what came next."
4. "Cold and heat, joy and pain: they come and go. Bear them. (2.14) Just this verse tonight."
5. "Fear not. That is what he said at the very end. (18.66)"
6. "Your mind is restless. Krishna agreed: it is hard to hold. And then he told Arjuna how. (6.35)"
7. "Five verses, and the lamp is lit. Rest now."
8. "You came back. The lamp was still warm."
9. "You spoke. A verse was listening. Here is what it says to you."
10. "That is enough for today. The verse will wait."

Style: sentences under 12 words, no exclamation marks, no emojis, Sanskrit first, meaning second, "you" third. Hindi and English versions are written, not machine translated.

## The vertical shloka scroll
- **One verse per screen**, centered, Sanskrit large (serif), transliteration small, meaning in second person.
- **Snap:** swipe up moves to the next verse with a soft settle (spring, ~380 ms). No half-states.
- **Hold to listen:** press and hold anywhere. Chant plays, words light up gold in time, a peacock-teal ring breathes. Release = pause.
- **Double-tap to keep:** a lotus blooms, soft "tink" and a light haptic. Kept verses appear in You > Kept.
- **No rail.** No like, comment, share icons. A thin dot row on top shows 3 of 5.
- **End of feed:** after verse 5, the screen dims: "Enough for today." One button to Walk. No "load more".
